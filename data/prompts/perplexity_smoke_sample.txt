This is a small smoke-test text sample used only to confirm that the Phase 1
quantization sweep pipeline runs end to end: model conversion, quantization,
benchmarking, and perplexity scoring all execute without error.

It is deliberately short and repetitive, which makes it unsuitable as an
actual perplexity benchmark. A model can trivially memorize or exploit its
repetition, so any perplexity number produced against this file is not
comparable across papers and must not be reported in Section 5.1 of the
article draft.

Before running the real Phase 1 sweep for reportable results, replace the
perplexity.corpus_path entry in config/phase1.yaml with a real held-out
corpus, such as the wikitext-2-raw test split (wiki.test.raw), which is the
standard corpus used for GGUF perplexity comparisons in the llama.cpp
ecosystem and will make the results comparable to published quantization
benchmarks.
